# ShopCo Category Owners

Effective date: 2025-04-01. Owner: Merchandising. Last reviewed: 2025-10-01.

## Role of a category owner

Each product category has one category owner in the Merchandising team. The category owner is accountable for the category's revenue target, its return rate, and supplier quality. Category owners receive the first escalation when their category breaches its maximum acceptable return rate (see returns_policy.md and escalation_process.md).
