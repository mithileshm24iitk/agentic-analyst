# ShopCo Discount and Promotion Rules

Effective date: 2025-01-01. Owner: Marketing. Last reviewed: 2025-10-05.

## General rules

Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with loyalty rewards, except the free shipping benefit of ShopCo Plus.

The maximum discount on any single item is 40% off retail_price. Any promotion that would discount an item by more than 40% needs written approval from the Head of Merchandising.

Discounts never apply to gift cards, shipping fees or taxes.

## Approved recurring promotions

Welcome offer: new customers get 15% off their first order, with a maximum discount of $30.
Spring Sale: 20% off all Swim, Shorts and Active items, from 2025-04-14 to 2025-04-27.
Back to School: 25% off Jeans, Pants and Tops & Tees, from 2025-08-04 to 2025-08-17.
Black Friday: 30% off sitewide, from 2025-11-28 to 2025-12-01.
End of Season Clearance: up to 40% off Outerwear & Coats and Sweaters, from 2026-01-05 to 2026-01-31.

## Excluded categories

Suits and Suits & Sport Coats are never part of sitewide promotions, including Black Friday. They may only be discounted through category-specific promotions approved by the Tailoring team.

## Promotions and returns

If a discounted item is returned, the refund equals the price the customer actually paid (sale_price), not the retail_price. If a return makes an order fall below a promotion's minimum spend, the promotion still stands; ShopCo does not claw back discounts.

## Approval process

New promotions must be submitted to Marketing at least 21 days before their start date. Promotions with an expected revenue impact over $100,000 also need sign-off from Finance.
