# ShopCo Plus Loyalty Program

Launch date: 2025-03-01. Owner: Customer Marketing. Last reviewed: 2025-10-12.

## Membership

ShopCo Plus is free to join. Any customer with an account can join. Members earn 1 point for every $1 of revenue they generate (revenue as defined in kpi_definitions.md, so returned and cancelled items earn no points).

## Tiers

Silver: 0 to 499 points in a calendar year. Benefit: free return shipping.
Gold: 500 to 1,499 points in a calendar year. Benefits: free return shipping and free standard shipping on every order.
Platinum: 1,500 or more points in a calendar year. Benefits: Gold benefits plus free expedited shipping and early access to the End of Season Clearance.

Tiers are recalculated on January 1 each year based on the previous calendar year's points.

## Rewards

Every 200 points can be redeemed for a $10 reward. Rewards expire 12 months after they are issued. Rewards cannot be combined with promo codes (see discounts_and_promotions.md).

## Program goals for 2026

The ShopCo Plus program targets a repeat customer rate of 35% among members by 2026-12-31, compared with a target of 25% for non-members. Repeat customer rate is defined in kpi_definitions.md.
