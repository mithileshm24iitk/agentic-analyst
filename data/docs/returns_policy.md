# ShopCo Returns Policy

Effective date: 2025-01-01. Owner: Customer Operations. Last reviewed: 2025-09-15.

## Customer return window

Customers may return any item within 30 days of the delivery date (order_items.delivered_at) for a full refund to the original payment method. Items returned between day 31 and day 60 receive store credit only. Items returned after day 60 are not accepted.

Items must be unworn, with original tags attached. Intimates, Underwear, Socks and Swim items must also be in their original sealed hygiene packaging.

Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at a distribution center.

## Return shipping costs

Return shipping is free for ShopCo Plus loyalty members and for items that arrived damaged or incorrect. All other customers pay a flat return shipping fee of $6.95, deducted from the refund.

## Maximum acceptable return rate per product category

ShopCo monitors the return rate of every product category each calendar month. The return rate is defined in kpi_definitions.md: the count of order_items with status 'Returned' divided by the count of all order_items for that category.

If a category's return rate is above its maximum acceptable return rate, the category is in breach and must be escalated as described in escalation_process.md.

## Fraud and abuse

Customers with more than 6 returns in any rolling 90-day period are flagged for review by the Trust & Safety team. Flagged customers may be limited to store credit refunds.
